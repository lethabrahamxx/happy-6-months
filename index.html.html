Data Storage Resolution

Why did your photos/text vanish earlier?
Standard phone photos are 5MB-15MB. Browsers strictly cap site storage to ~5MB total. Storing uncompressed photos instantly caused silent browser quota errors!

Two Solutions Applied:

    1. Canvas Compression: Photos are automatically downscaled to max 800px & compressed to ~100KB JPEG on upload. 2. Standalone HTML Exporter: Download a fully hardcoded `.html` file that embeds your customized base64 photos & letter so it works forever on any phone or PC!
Memory saved safely!
